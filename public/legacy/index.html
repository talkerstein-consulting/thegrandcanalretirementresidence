    Why us?

    The Grand Canal Retirement Residence stands out as a premier retirement community, offering exceptional care and services for seniors. Our commitment to providing a comfortable and enjoyable lifestyle for our residents is evident in the numerous amenities and services we offer, including 24/7 on-site registered medical staff, customizable care plans, chef-prepared gourmet meals and a range of activities to promote physical and mental well-being.

    ​

    Tour today and enjoy the unparalleled ambiance, insatiable views and most of all what many graciously express what they “feel” with the absolute natural warmth shared by our lovely residents and delightful staff.  The genuine comradery, pleasure in living life their way is natural each day.  The intangibility of the “feel” is why most decide to call us home.  Yours to discover and to start living life as you deserve

    LOCATION

    We are located on the banks of the Welland Recreational Waterway, in the heart of Welland, ON. Living at the Grand Canal means nature is at your fingertips. We feature easy access to breathtaking canal views, walking trails, and parks. Shopping, entertainment, sports, and fitness facilities are nearby.

    The Grand Canal has indoor access to the Welland Community Centre opening up year-round access to many activities and entertainment.
