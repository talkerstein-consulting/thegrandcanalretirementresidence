    DINING

    Delicious Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner Options for Seniors

    Food is the international language of friendship! Since we require food to live, why not indulge in the gourmet dining experience of The Grand Canal Retirement Residence? If you’re looking to take your tastebuds on an adventure, the delicious meals and snacks provided by our dining service stand out from the fold.

    Our Dining Services

    Our dining services are second to none as our culinary team is renown for its talent and expertise. When searching for retirement homes in Niagara, it’s important to find a community that not only prioritizes healthy meal plans, but also the dining experience. At The Grand Canal, we do just that. Not only are our meals healthy and delicious, but we provide a culinary experience like no other at our onsite restaurant.

    Canal View Restaurant & Seasonal Patio Dining

    Chef-Prepared Food/Menu & Restaurant Style Dining

    Situated along the banks of the Welland Recreational Waterway, the restaurant at The Grand Canal is the heart and soul of our senior community. The friendly atmosphere and prompt courteous service of our wait staff will have you feeling like royalty! Sit back, enjoy the spectacular views of the local waterway and natural scenery as you chat with new friends

    ​

    During the warmer months, indulge in our delicious chef-prepared meals while basking in the serene and tranquil nature of the canal. Our seasonal patio dining is the perfect place to spend an unforgettable mealtime in the company of your loved ones.

    Choose from an array of culinary options from our full restaurant-style menu, all prepared by our highly qualified chef and culinary team. The menu offers nutritious well-balanced meals—from various appetizers to sides, desserts, soups, fruit, and entrees for each meal—all prepared fresh daily. We also have daily features for lunch and dinner, as well as upscale menu options such as a AAA Canadian beef sirloin steak or salmon. The options are endless! We believe that our residents should have a choice in what they eat. So, indulge in what you heart desires!

    Full-Service Bar

    In addition to our restaurant-stye menu, The Grand Canal offers full bar service during each meal. Whether you’re looking to pair your dinner with the perfect glass of Merlot, or you feel like having a fancy cocktail…the choice is yours! Let’s not forget the aroma of freshly brewed coffee and the best cup of tea on this side of the Atlantic Ocean! We always have coffee and tea available to our residents.

    Open Seating and Expanded Mealtimes

    At The Grand Canal Retirement Residence, we are proud to provide open seating in the restaurant. This enables everyone to enjoy their meals with family or friends without feeling rushed or crowded. The restaurant also offers extended mealtimes to avoid having a regimented meal schedule, meaning residents can stay as long as they’d like.

    Post-Dinner Nightcap at the Pub

    Continental Breakfast

    With the cozy atmosphere of the pub, make plans to meet friends for pre-dinner cocktails as you decide what your taste buds are craving for dinner. After dinner, enjoy a nightcap with others while you make plans for the next day’s adventures. Choose from the pub’s wide selection of fine wines and spirits.

    Start the day on the right side of the bed with a full continental breakfast of fruits, pastries, baked goods, toast, tea, oatmeal, cereal, or coffee in the bistro section. Sample the fresh food selection while gazing at the natural beauty of the area, known as a top pick for retirement living near Font Hill, Niagara Falls, St. Catharines and beyond.

    Private Dining Area

    We understand that sometimes our residents want a more intimate dining experience. That's why we offer a private dining area that is perfect for small groups, family gatherings, or special occasions.

    ​

    Our private dining area provides an elegant and exclusive setting where you can enjoy our chef-prepared meals with your loved ones in a more intimate setting. Whether you're celebrating a milestone or just looking to share a special meal with friends and family, our private dining area is the perfect place to do so.

    Craft kitchen

    Our craft kitchen is a unique feature at The Grand Canal Retirement Residence. It is the perfect place for residents to indulge in their passion for cooking, baking, and crafting.

     

    Our fully-equipped kitchen provides everything residents need to create delicious and beautiful dishes, from professional-grade appliances to a variety of cooking and baking tools. The craft kitchen is a wonderful place for residents to connect with each other, share recipes and cooking tips, and explore new culinary adventures together.

    Complimentary Beverages and Snacks 24/7

    As the hunger pains and cravings can hit at odd hours, a 24/7 café service is available. Choose from an assortment of complimentary beverages and snacks. Have an unexpected guest pop by for a visit? No problem as the café is always stocked with delicious treats!

     

    To book a tour of our beautiful retirement community or to learn more about our dining experience, contact us at (289) 480-0125 or fill out the form on our website. Our entire team, especially our friendly waiters, invite you to eat like kings and queens!
