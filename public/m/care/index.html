CARE OPTIONS

24/7 ON-SITE REGISTERED MEDICAL STAFF

At The Grand Canal Retirement Residence, we are committed to providing the highest level of care for our residents. That's why we have 24/7 on-site registered medical staff available to provide care and support whenever it's needed. Our medical staff is highly trained and experienced, with a focus on promoting health and wellness and ensuring the safety and well-being of our residents. With around-the-clock care, our residents can have peace of mind knowing that they are always in good hands.

CUSTOMIZED CARE PLANS

At The Grand Canal Retirement Residence, we believe that aging in place should be comfortable, convenient, and flexible. That's why we offer customizable care plans that allow our residents to age in place while receiving the support they need to maintain their independence and quality of life. Our personalized care plans are designed to adapt and change with our residents, so they can receive the care they need at every stage of their lives. Whether it's assistance with daily activities, medication management, or specialized care, we work with our residents to ensure that they receive the support they need to thrive in a comfortable and familiar environment.

EMERGENCY PENDANT RESPONSE SYSTEM

Providing an emergency pendant response system is a critical component of our commitment to ensuring the safety and well-being of our residents. We understand that emergencies can happen at any time, and we want to ensure that our residents can receive prompt assistance if they need it. 

Our emergency pendant response system allows our residents to quickly and easily call for help if they experience a medical emergency or other urgent situation. The pendant is worn around the neck or on the wrist, and when the button is pressed, a signal is sent to our staff who can immediately respond to the situation.

PHARMACY SERVICES

Our Doctor and Nurses are in-touch with our local pharmacy and precriptions are managed by our nursing department and delivered to our retirement home on a regular basis.

Our residents are therefore not required to travel to the pharmacy and can rest assured that their health & wellness is completely taken care of from within the retirement home.

DOCTORS SERVICES

Our Doctor is scheduled regularly to support our medical and holistic needs. Having a doctor personally attend on-site at the retirement home is a great benefit to our residents. This can help to ensure that our residents receive regular medical check-ups and can access medical care more quickly and easily.
