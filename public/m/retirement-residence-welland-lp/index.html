WELCOME TO THE

GRAND CANAL RETIREMENT RESIDENCE

Come join us at the Grand Canal - a new take on Independent Seniors Retirement Living in Welland, Ontario. The Grand Canal features exceptional views of the Welland Recreational Canal. 

SUITES

The Grand Canal offers a range of meticulously designed, and well-crafted suites in a variety of floor plans, sizes, and prices to meet your needs.

​

Come enjoy spectacular views from our Studio:

  • 1-Bedroom

  • 1-Bedroom+Den

  • 2-Bedroom suites

LOCATION

We are located on the banks of the Welland Recreational Waterway, in the heart of Welland, ON. Living at the Grand Canal means nature is at your fingertips. We feature easy access to breathtaking canal views, walking trails, and parks. Shopping, entertainment, sports, and fitness facilities are nearby.

​

The Grand Canal has indoor access to the Welland Community Centre opening up year-round access to many activities and entertainment.

SERVICES

HOUSEKEEPING AND LAUNDRY

Weekly housekeeping and linen laundering services are included, and we’ll provide extra services, such as personal laundry, daily tidy or spring cleaning, for a small fee.

SALON SERVICES

Our salon offers a host of services, including treatments, to help you look and feel your best.

PET SERVICES

We are committed to helping seniors and their pets stay together. Ask about our pet feeding, housekeeping, and dog walking services.

SECURITY

Safety and security are our top priorities, and we have registered staff in the building 24/7.

SOCIAL AND RECREATION PROGRAMS

Free recreation, learning, and social programs. Membership to the Welland Community Wellness Complex is included with easy indoor access.

  • Featuring Therapeutic Pool & Hot Tub

  • Exercise & Activity Room

  • Library

  • Woodworking Room

  • 400-Seat Theatre

  • Over 80 different programs & 25 drop-in activities

Transportation to and from recreation events.

PERSONALIZED CARE SERVICES

  • Medication Support 

  • 24/7 Registered Staff

  • Emergency Call Bell Response System

  • Personal Care such as bathing and dressing assistance and more

  • A variety of other Nursing & Personal Care services

AMENITIES

Life at Grand Canal is all about lifestyle. Our community features delightful amenity spaces, each with spectacular views of the Canal. Invite your friends for a game of pool in our Games Room or a drink in our Pub. Making new friends is easy here when you join free classes in the Craft Kitchen, take in the water views on our Patio or decide to hang-out in our Bistro. 

DINING

Whether you’re looking for a quick bite to eat, want to spend time with friends over a delicious meal, or want to cook dinner with your family, at the Grand Canal you’ll be treated to a range of gourmet dining experiences.  

WHO WE ARE

The Grand Canal is led by a team of highly skilled and friendly professionals focused on providing exceptional experiences for you, your family and friends. We believe aging is an adventure.

Life at the Grand Canal is about making new friends, living with purpose and valuing the things that matter in life.  We offer to help you truly live the next chapter of your life.
