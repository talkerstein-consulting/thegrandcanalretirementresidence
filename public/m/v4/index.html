Questions

Frequently asked questions

Care and costs

Is The Grand Canal a nursing home?

No. The Grand Canal is an independent retirement residence, with care services available as needed.

What is included each month?

Monthly accommodation includes meals, housekeeping, utilities and activities. Care services are quoted separately, and personal laundry is an additional service.

What happens if more support is needed?

Additional care is arranged as needs change. Care costs are reviewed with the resident and family together with our nursing department.

Can residents keep their own doctor?

Yes. A visiting physician is available and accepting new patients, or residents may continue care with their current doctor.

Is there staff on duty at all times?

Yes. Registered staff are on site 24/7.

Is the residence secure?

Yes. The building has secured access, building rounds seven days a week, and room checks if required.

Are short stays available?

At this time, we do not offer short-term or respite stays.

Daily life

What does independent supportive living mean?

Independent living with included services such as meals, housekeeping and a variety of engaging activities. Additional care services are also available to support changing needs and help residents age in place with comfort and confidence.

Do you provide diet plans?

Meals are freshly prepared and served restaurant-style, chosen to your personal diet and taste.

What furniture and appliances are included?

Residents bring their own furniture. Each suite has a kitchenette with a full-size refrigerator and microwave, and in-suite air conditioning and heating.

Do you allow pets?

Yes. Our residence is pet friendly, and we welcome your furry friends to feel right at home.

When can family and friends visit?

Any time. Banquet rooms are available for larger gatherings.

Can I go out any time?

Yes. Residents come and go as they please.

Is there parking?

On-site parking is available for residents and visitors, with reserved spaces also offered.
