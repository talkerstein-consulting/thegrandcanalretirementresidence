Care services

A continuum of care

Every resident has access to registered staff on site 24 hours a day. Care plans are customized to each resident and adapt as needs change, so residents can age in place with comfort and confidence.

  • ✓Registered nursing staff, 24 hours a day

  • ✓Mobility support
  • ✓Extended support hours
  • ✓Care plans customized to each resident
  • ✓A doctor who visits on site regularly, with prescriptions managed by the nursing team
