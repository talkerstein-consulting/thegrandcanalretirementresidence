Why us?

A premier retirement community on the water

The Grand Canal stands out for exceptional care and services for seniors: 24/7 on-site registered medical staff, customizable care plans, chef-prepared gourmet meals and activities that keep body and mind well.

Tour today and enjoy the ambiance, the views and, most of all, what residents describe as the “feel”: the natural warmth shared by our residents and staff. The comradery and the pleasure of living life your way are part of every day. That feel is why most decide to call us home.

Location

On the banks of the Welland Recreational Waterway

Living at the Grand Canal means nature is at your fingertips: canal views, walking trails and parks, with shopping, entertainment, sports and fitness nearby.

Indoor access to the Welland Community Wellness Centre opens up year-round activities and entertainment.
